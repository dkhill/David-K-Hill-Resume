# About Me
I’m the kind of engineer who doesn’t just bring order to chaos — I make the chaos document itself, version‑control its own behavior, and politely explain what went wrong before it ever becomes a ticket. As a Senior Model‑Based Systems Engineer and ITSM Automation Architect, I build infrastructures that are self‑explanatory, resilient, and ready for audit without anyone breaking a sweat. Whether I’m crafting digital twins, designing automation pipelines that behave like responsible adults, or wrestling cross‑platform sync issues into submission with a well‑placed rsync flag, I approach every challenge with the same philosophy: systems should be teachable, traceable, and durable enough to outlive their creators.

Outside the technical trenches, I channel that same structured creativity into my hobbies. I love experimenting in the kitchen and turning fusion recipes into repeatable, documented processes — because even dinner deserves a proper workflow. I also dive into hands‑on electronics projects, from microcontroller builds to small‑scale automation experiments, always looking for ways to blend hardware, software, and systems thinking into something elegant. Whether I’m refining a recipe, prototyping a circuit, or exploring new places to live, I’m happiest when I’m building something that’s both functional and enduring.
